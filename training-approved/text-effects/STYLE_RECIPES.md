# Original text-effect construction recipes

Original, general-purpose descriptions authored for this repository. These recipes are **not** descriptions of Magnific thumbnails.

## Chrome with vivid yellow reflection

Background: dark navy/charcoal. Display type: geometric extra-bold, wide tracking. Shape: silver face, medium-gray beveled edge and dark charcoal extrusion. Light: narrow white specular streak across upper faces, a bright golden-yellow reflection band across lower-middle faces, smaller cyan rim reflection. Build separated masks or SVG gradients so reflections follow the letter shape, not a rectangular color overlay. Keep 3D shadows coherent.

## Clean neon cyan

Background: midnight indigo. Display type: heavy, spacious, simple silhouette. Letter face: nearly white cyan. Add one sharp neon edge, a broader diffuse turquoise glow, and a very faint cyan reflection on nearby surfaces. The bright sharp core should remain legible at mobile sizes.

## Warm gold medal

Background: deep aubergine or black. Display type: elegant slab-serif or a carefully spaced geometric sans. Letter face: bands of champagne, amber and burnt gold. Dark brown extrusion and a tight cream highlight suggest polished metal; maintain believable material direction.

## Checklist

- Readable silhouette when reduced to 20% size.
- Strong intentional light source.
- Distinct highlight, midtone and shadow families.
- Limited number of finishes per composition.
- No cut-off bevel, broken baseline or visually inconsistent extrusion.
- Original typography and artwork; no third-party stock material.
